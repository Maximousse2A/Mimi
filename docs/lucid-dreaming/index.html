Private dream practice

Remember your dreams. Practice lucid dreaming gently.

Lucid Dreaming combines a private on-device journal, short lessons, mindful reality checks, reminders, and progress reflections without interpreting what your dreams mean.

Privacy Policy Get Support

Designed around privacy

Your journal stays with you.

Local journal

Dream text, tags, reflections, and optional voice recordings are stored on your device. Lucid Dreaming does not upload them to an operator-run server.

No account

You can use the app without creating an account. Your practice profile and progress remain local to the app.

No dream interpretation

The app helps you observe your own patterns and learn established practices. It does not assign fixed meanings or diagnose sleep or health conditions.

Lucid Dreaming is an educational wellbeing tool, not medical care. Stop or reduce night practices if they disrupt your sleep or wellbeing, and consult a qualified professional about persistent sleep problems or distressing dreams.
