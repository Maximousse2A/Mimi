Helpful details to include

  • Your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and Lucid Dreaming app version.

  • Whether the issue involves journaling, audio, reminders, journal lock, export, practice, progress, or purchases.
  • A screenshot if it does not contain dream content or other information you prefer not to share.

Subscriptions and purchases

To manage or cancel Dreamer Plus, open Apple subscription settings. To restore an eligible purchase, choose “Restore purchases” in Lucid Dreaming. Apple handles billing and refund decisions.

Microphone, notifications, or journal lock

Open iOS Settings, choose Lucid Dreaming, and review the permission you want to change. Face ID, Touch ID, and device-passcode decisions are handled by iOS.

Local data and recovery

Lucid Dreaming has no account or cloud journal. SantaLucia cannot retrieve journal entries or recordings from your device. Before deleting the app, use the PDF export if you want a readable copy. Uninstalling the app does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Safety note

Lucid Dreaming is educational and is not medical care. Stop night practices if they disrupt your sleep or wellbeing. Seek help from a qualified professional for persistent sleep problems or distressing dreams, and use local emergency services for emergencies.
