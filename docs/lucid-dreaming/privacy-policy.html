Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Lucid Dreaming iOS app, visit this website, or contact support.

Who operates Lucid Dreaming

Lucid Dreaming is operated by SantaLucia. For privacy questions or requests, email maxime.pietri@icloud.com.

Information stored on your device

Journal and recordings

Dream titles, journal text, dates, recall and clarity ratings, lucid status, emotions, tags, dream signs, favorites, and optional audio recordings are stored in the app container on your device. Lucid Dreaming does not send this content to SantaLucia, Superwall, or an AI service.

Profile, wellbeing answers, and progress

Your optional preferred name, goals, experience level, sleep rhythm, self-reported sleep quality, difficult-dream frequency, practice history, reality-check history, lesson progress, bookmarks, reminder schedules, and app preferences are stored locally. On-device rules may use these values to adapt the practice and safety guidance. They are not used to diagnose a condition or infer the meaning of a dream.

Microphone, notifications, and device authentication

Microphone access is requested only when you choose to record audio. Notification access is requested only when you enable reminders. If you enable the journal lock, Face ID, Touch ID, or the device passcode is evaluated by iOS; Lucid Dreaming does not receive or store biometric data. You can change these permissions in iOS Settings.

PDF exports

Journal PDFs are created locally. A copy leaves the protected app container only when you explicitly use the iOS share sheet, after which the destination you select controls that copy.

Information processed by service providers

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple StoreKit processes App Store products, payments, subscription status, purchase restoration, refunds, and related transaction information. SantaLucia does not receive your full payment-card details.

Paywalls and related analytics

Lucid Dreaming uses Superwall to download and present paywalls and to understand paywall and subscription performance. Superwall may process an anonymous app-user or device identifier, vendor identifier, app version, operating system, device model, locale, IP-derived approximate region, paywall and placement interactions, product identifiers, purchase or subscription status, and technical diagnostics. Lucid Dreaming does not intentionally send journal text, recordings, dream tags, wellbeing answers, or your preferred name to Superwall.

Website and support

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web-request information such as IP address, browser or device information, requested pages, and timestamps for hosting, security, and operational purposes. This site does not add its own advertising or analytics cookies. If you email support, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send.

How information is used

  • Provide local journaling, recordings, lessons, reminders, progress, exports, and safety features.
  • Present subscription offers, process and restore purchases, and determine premium access.
  • Measure paywall and subscription performance and diagnose related technical problems.
  • Host these public support and legal pages.
  • Respond to support, privacy, legal, and security requests.

Sharing and service providers

Lucid Dreaming relies on Apple, Superwall, GitHub Pages, and email providers for the functions described above. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We require service providers acting for Lucid Dreaming to handle information consistently with this policy and applicable law. We do not sell or rent your journal, recordings, profile, or support messages, and we do not use them for third-party advertising.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete individual content where controls are available or uninstall Lucid Dreaming. Uninstalling removes the app's local container, subject to how iOS and any device backup you control operate. Because Lucid Dreaming has no user account or SantaLucia-operated cloud journal, SantaLucia cannot retrieve or remotely delete local journal data.

Support messages are retained only as long as reasonably needed to answer your request and maintain appropriate security or legal records. Apple, Superwall, GitHub, and email providers retain information according to their settings, agreements, and legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of support information we control.

Your choices and rights

  • Manage microphone, notification, and biometric permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Delete individual journal entries or uninstall the app to remove its local data.
  • Export selected journal information as a PDF before deleting the app.
  • Manage or cancel Dreamer Plus in your Apple ID subscription settings.
  • Email us to request access, correction, or deletion of support information we control.

See Privacy Choices for practical instructions.

Children

Lucid Dreaming is not directed to children under 13 and is not offered in the App Store Kids category.

International processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their applicable privacy terms describe the safeguards they use for international transfers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, its providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version and update date will remain available at this URL.
