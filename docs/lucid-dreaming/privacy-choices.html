Privacy Choices

Lucid Dreaming gives you practical ways to control device permissions, local information, exports, and subscriptions.

Microphone, notifications, and journal lock

Open iOS Settings, choose Lucid Dreaming, and change microphone, notification, or Face ID permissions at any time. Without microphone permission, you can still create text entries. Without notifications, the journal and practice remain usable.

Local journal and practice data

Delete individual dream entries using the journal's delete controls. Uninstalling Lucid Dreaming removes its local app container, including journal content, recordings, profile, practice history, and preferences. SantaLucia cannot recover this local data after deletion.

Export before deletion

You may create and share a journal PDF from Profile & Settings before deleting the app. The service or location you select in the iOS share sheet then controls that exported copy.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel Dreamer Plus in Apple subscription settings. Use “Restore purchases” inside Lucid Dreaming to restore an eligible purchase. Deleting the app does not automatically cancel an active Apple subscription.

Support and privacy requests

Lucid Dreaming does not operate user accounts or a cloud journal database. For access, correction, or deletion requests concerning support messages we control, email maxime.pietri@icloud.com.
